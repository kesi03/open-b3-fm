OpenB3 / setBfree Beatrix attribution
=====================================

This firmware contains a freestanding port of the Beatrix C audio engine from
OpenB3 by Michele Perrone (https://github.com/michele-perrone/OpenB3).

OpenB3 source used:
  BeatrixCPP/Source/tonegen/*
  BeatrixCPP/Source/vibrato/*
  BeatrixCPP/Source/reverb/*
  BeatrixCPP/Source/whirl/*
  BeatrixCPP/Source/overdrive/*
  and the corresponding headers / global_definitions.
