On the hardware: hold SHIFT and turn A/B, or press SHIFT + ◀ / ▶, to browse the same presets. The name is in the top right of the display.

Device display

Drums are always opt-in: tap pads 1–8 or the DRUMS toggle to start; DAW Play and Record never start them. Pads 9–16 play chord stabs. The mixer has four slots — upper, lower, pedal and drums — each with its own level. Keys and MIDI play the upper manual; MIDI channel 2 the lower, channel 3 the pedals.
